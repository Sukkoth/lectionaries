================================================================================
           HOW TO SET UP THE DAILY LECTIONARY BOT IN YOUR CHANNEL OR GROUP
================================================================================

Welcome! This guide shows you how to add the Daily Lectionary Bot to your 
Telegram Channel or Group to receive daily Bible readings and liturgical 
calendars automatically every morning.

You can customize everything (language, Bible version, and posting time) 
privately with the bot without sending any commands or cluttering your channel!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. ADDING THE BOT TO YOUR TELEGRAM CHANNEL (Recommended)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Follow these simple steps:

Step 1: Open your Telegram Channel.
Step 2: Tap the channel name at the top to open Channel Info.
Step 3: Tap "Administrators", then tap "Add Administrator".
Step 4: Search for the bot by its username and select it.
Step 5: Make sure the permission "Post Messages" is turned ON.
Step 6: Tap the checkmark to save.

Done! The bot is now connected to your channel.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ADDING THE BOT TO YOUR TELEGRAM GROUP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Step 1: Open your Telegram Group.
Step 2: Tap the group name at the top, then tap "Add Members".
Step 3: Search for the bot by its username and add it.
Step 4: (Optional but recommended) Promote the bot to Administrator so it can 
        post reliably without message restrictions.

Done! The bot will greet the group and start sharing daily readings.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. SILENT CONFIGURATION (How to customize from your private chat)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

You do NOT need to type commands inside your public channel. You can configure 
your channel or group quietly from a private chat with the bot!

Step 1: Open a private chat with the bot.
Step 2: Tap START or send /start.
Step 3: Send any of the configuration commands below, including your channel 
        or group username (e.g., @my_church_channel):

• Change Language for your channel:
  /setlanguage @my_channel_username am
  /setlanguage @my_channel_username om
  /setlanguage @my_channel_username en

• Change Bible Version for your channel:
  /setversion @my_channel_username am54
  /setversion @my_channel_username esv
  /setversion @my_channel_username macqul

• Change Daily Posting Time for your channel:
  /settime @my_channel_username 06:00
  /settime @my_channel_username 07:30
  /settime @my_channel_username 08:30

• Preview today's post for your channel:
  /today @my_channel_username

* Note: You must be an Administrator of the channel or group for the bot to 
  accept your settings.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. CONFIGURING DIRECTLY INSIDE A GROUP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

If you prefer configuring inside a group, any group admin can send:

• /settings     - Opens an interactive menu with buttons to change language, 
                  version, and posting time.
• /today        - Shows today's full Bible readings immediately.
• /setlanguage  - Set language (e.g. /setlanguage am)
• /setversion   - Set Bible version (e.g. /setversion am54)
• /settime      - Set daily posting time (e.g. /settime 08:00)
• /app          - Link to download the mobile app on Android.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. SUPPORTED LANGUAGES & BIBLE VERSIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Supported Languages:
• Amharic (code: am)
• Afaan Oromoo (code: om)
• English (code: en)
• Kambaata (code: ktb)
• Hadiyya (code: hdy)
• Sidama (code: sidamo)
• Tigrinya (code: ትግርኛ)

Popular Bible Versions:
• am54    - 1954 Amharic Bible (default for Amharic)
• esv     - English Standard Version (default for English)
• niv     - New International Version
• macqul  - Macaafa Qulqulluu (Afaan Oromoo)
• nasv    - New Amharic Standard Version

Posting Times:
• Times are set in 24-hour format in local Ethiopian Time (UTC+3 / EAT).
• Choose any hour or half-hour: 06:00, 06:30, 07:00, 07:30, 08:00, 08:30, etc.
• Default time: 08:30 in the morning.
